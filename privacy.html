Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SintBot ("we", "us") operates the website sintbot.com and publishes two pieces of open-source software: sintbot, the self-hosted daemon, and cc-bot, the Claude Code plugin. This policy explains what little data the site touches, and what the software does and does not send. We keep it deliberately minimal.

No accounts, no direct collection

The website has no login, no account system, and no forms. We do not directly collect names, emails, or other personal information when you browse the site.

Anonymous analytics

We use Umami for privacy-first, aggregate analytics. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not collect personally identifiable information. We only see anonymous, aggregated stats such as page views and referrers.

Local storage

Your browser's localStorage is used only to remember your language preference (English or Chinese). Nothing is sent to us.

The cc-bot software

The cc-bot software runs entirely on your own machine. It does not send your code, credentials, or data to us. It communicates only with the services you configure (such as your Slack or Lark workspace and the AI models you connect).

The sintbot daemon

The sintbot daemon runs on your own machines. We run no server for it and receive nothing from it: no telemetry, no usage data. Its records — sessions, run logs, usage counters — are written on the machine it runs on and stay there. What leaves that machine goes only to the services you set up: prompts, group messages and the files and command output the model reads go to the model provider you chose for that group (through the claude or codex command-line tools, whose own behavior is up to their providers); messages, files and cards go through your chat platform; quota and balance lookups for the console use the logins or keys already on the machine. Its local console page loads fonts from Google Fonts in the browser that opens it. Outgoing messages are redacted first, masking strings that look like secrets before they reach your workspace.

Your workspace provider and your chosen AI providers handle data under their own privacy policies. Which providers see your data is entirely determined by how you configure the daemon.

The desktop pet

The desktop pet mentioned on our site is in development and is not yet released or distributed. It collects nothing today. If and when it ships, this policy will be updated before it is made available.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@sintbot.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 17 日

SintBot（「我们」）运营网站 sintbot.com，并发布两个开源软件：自托管守护进程 sintbot 与 Claude Code 插件 cc-bot。本政策说明本站接触到的极少量数据，以及这些软件会与不会发送什么。我们刻意把它做到最简。

无账号、不直接收集

本站没有登录、没有账号系统、没有表单。你浏览本站时，我们不会直接收集姓名、邮箱或其他个人信息。

匿名统计

我们使用 Umami 做隐私优先的聚合统计。它不设 cookie、不跨站追踪、不收集可识别个人身份的信息。我们只看到匿名的聚合数据，比如页面浏览量和来源。

本地存储

浏览器的 localStorage 仅用于记住你的语言偏好（英文或中文），不会向我们发送任何内容。

cc-bot 软件

cc-bot 软件完全运行在你自己的机器上，不会把你的代码、凭证或数据传给我们。它只与你自己配置的服务通信（比如你的 Slack / 飞书 workspace 和你接入的 AI 模型）。

sintbot 守护进程

sintbot 守护进程运行在你自己的机器上。我们没有为它运行任何服务器，也不从它接收任何东西：没有遥测、没有使用数据。它产生的记录——会话、运行日志、用量计数——写在它所运行的那台机器上并留在那里。会离开这台机器的只发往你自己设置的服务：每一轮的提示词、群消息以及模型读到的文件与命令输出，经 claude 或 codex 命令行发给你给这个群选的模型服务商（这两个命令行自身的行为由其提供方决定）；消息、文件与卡片经你的聊天平台收发；控制台查询额度与余额时，用的是这台机器上已有的登录态或 key。它的本地控制台页面会在打开它的浏览器里从 Google Fonts 加载字体。出站消息会先经过脱敏，把疑似密钥的字符串遮掉，再发进你的 workspace。

你的 workspace 服务商与你选用的 AI 服务商，按它们各自的隐私政策处理数据。哪些服务商会看到你的数据，完全由你如何配置这个守护进程决定。

桌宠

本站提到的桌宠仍在研发中，尚未发布、也未对外分发，目前不收集任何数据。若将来上线，我们会在它对外提供之前先更新本政策。
