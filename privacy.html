Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

SintBot ("we", "us") operates the website sintbot.com, the open-source cc-bot software, and sintbot — the self-hosted daemon delivered with SintBot Pro. This policy explains what little data the site touches, how purchases are handled, and what the software you run does and does not send us. We keep it deliberately minimal.

No accounts, no direct collection

The website has no login, no account system, and no forms. We do not directly collect names, emails, or other personal information when you browse the site.

Anonymous analytics

We use Umami for privacy-first, aggregate analytics. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not collect personally identifiable information. We only see anonymous, aggregated stats such as page views and referrers.

Local storage

Your browser's localStorage is used only to remember your language preference (English or Chinese). Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases (SintBot Pro)

When you subscribe to SintBot Pro, payment is processed by Creem acting as our Merchant of Record. Your name, email, and payment details are submitted to and handled by Creem under their privacy policy: creem.io/privacy. We receive only the information needed to deliver support and manage your subscription.

The cc-bot software

The cc-bot software runs entirely on your own machine. It does not send your code, credentials, or data to us. It communicates only with the services you configure (such as your Slack or Lark workspace and the AI models you connect).

The sintbot daemon

The sintbot daemon runs on your own machines. It does not send your code, credentials, conversations, or project data to us. It talks only to the services you configure: your Lark or Slack workspace, the AI model endpoints you point it at, and whatever tools you enable for it. Its records — chat sessions, run logs, usage counters — are written to the machine it runs on and stay there; we have no access to them and no telemetry is sent back. Outbound messages pass through a redaction step that masks secret-shaped strings before anything is posted to your workspace.

Your workspace provider and your chosen AI providers handle data under their own privacy policies. Which providers see your data is entirely determined by how you configure the daemon.

The desktop pet

The desktop pet shown on our site is in development and is not yet released or distributed. It collects nothing today. If and when it ships, this policy will be updated before it is made available.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@sintbot.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 5 日

SintBot（「我们」）运营网站 sintbot.com、开源软件 cc-bot，以及随 SintBot Pro 交付的自托管守护进程 sintbot。本政策说明本站接触到的极少量数据、购买流程如何处理，以及你运行的软件会与不会把什么发给我们。我们刻意把它做到最简。

无账号、不直接收集

本站没有登录、没有账号系统、没有表单。你浏览本站时，我们不会直接收集姓名、邮箱或其他个人信息。

匿名统计

我们使用 Umami 做隐私优先的聚合统计。它不设 cookie、不跨站追踪、不收集可识别个人身份的信息。我们只看到匿名的聚合数据，比如页面浏览量和来源。

本地存储

浏览器的 localStorage 仅用于记住你的语言偏好（英文或中文），不会向我们发送任何内容。

购买（SintBot Pro）

订阅 SintBot Pro 时，支付由 Creem 作为 Merchant of Record（卖方记录方）处理。你的姓名、邮箱和支付信息提交给 Creem，并按其隐私政策处理：creem.io/privacy。我们只接收提供支持和管理订阅所必需的信息。

cc-bot 软件

cc-bot 软件完全运行在你自己的机器上，不会把你的代码、凭证或数据传给我们。它只与你自己配置的服务通信（比如你的 Slack / 飞书 workspace 和你接入的 AI 模型）。

sintbot 守护进程

sintbot 守护进程运行在你自己的机器上。它不会把你的代码、凭证、对话或项目数据传给我们。它只与你自己配置的服务通信：你的飞书或 Slack workspace、你指定的 AI 模型端点，以及你为它启用的工具。它产生的记录——会话、运行日志、用量计数——写在它所运行的那台机器上并留在那里，我们无从访问，也不会有任何遥测回传。出站消息会先经过脱敏，把疑似密钥的字符串遮掉，再发进你的 workspace。

你的 workspace 服务商与你选用的 AI 服务商，按它们各自的隐私政策处理数据。哪些服务商会看到你的数据，完全由你如何配置这个守护进程决定。

桌宠

本站展示的桌宠仍在研发中，尚未发布、也未对外分发，目前不收集任何数据。若将来上线，我们会在它对外提供之前先更新本政策。

联系

有隐私方面的问题？请写信至 support@sintbot.com。
